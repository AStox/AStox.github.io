Questions

How do I remove ads?

Open the Shop and buy Remove Ads, or Knot Fun Complete (no ads and every theme unlocked). Each is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions.

I reinstalled or got a new phone and my purchase is gone.

Open the Shop and tap "restore purchases" while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your progress and stats.

When do ads show?

Never during a knot. Your first 3 knots have no ads, then an ad can show between knots, at most one every few minutes. The "ad break" on the Home screen lets you watch one ad to skip ads for your next 3 knots, once a day.

How do I report an ad?

Email us with a screenshot of the ad, or a description of it and roughly when you saw it, so we can block it.

How do themes unlock?

By playing. Themes unlock for free as you untangle knots, and the Knots tab shows what unlocks each one. Knot Fun Complete unlocks all of them at once.

What is the Daily Knot?

One expert knot a day, the same for everyone. It opens after about 10 completed knots.

How do I share a knot?

After you untangle a knot, tap "share your knot". A friend who taps your link plays the same knot. If the link opens a web page instead of the game, install Knot Fun and tap the link again.

How do I turn off usage stats or ad tracking?

Usage stats: tap the gear on the Home screen and switch off "share anonymous usage stats". Ad tracking: in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. Where the law requires it, the Shop also has "privacy choices" for Google's ad consent.
