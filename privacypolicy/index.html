Privacy Policy

Knot Fun · Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains what the Knot Fun app does with your data. The short version: Knot Fun has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google, sends anonymous usage stats to PostHog (you can turn them off), and lets Apple handle purchases.

Who we are

Knot Fun is made by Adam Stockermans, an independent developer ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the data described here. For any question about this policy or your data, email adamstox@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your progress, points, stats, unlocked themes, settings, a knot in progress, and which purchases you own are saved in the app's own storage on your device. We don't upload it. A few numbers from it (knots untied, points, level and which purchases you own) are part of the usage stats below, when those are on. Like any app data, it can be included in your device backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Usage stats (PostHog)

Knot Fun collects anonymous usage statistics to understand how the game is played and to make it better: which screens and buttons are used, how knots are started, finished or left, purchases and ad views, settings, and crash reports, together with the app version, device model, operating system, screen size and language. These statistics are tied only to a random number created when the app is installed, never to your name, email, advertising identifier or location, and your IP address is not stored. They are processed by PostHog, Inc. on servers in the European Union and are not sold or used for advertising.

Your IP address is seen when the stats are sent, as with any internet connection, but the game tells PostHog not to store it and not to use it to look up your location. We don't create a profile of you. See PostHog's privacy policy.

Turning stats off. Tap the gear on the Home screen to open Settings and switch off "share anonymous usage stats". While it is off nothing is sent, and any stats waiting on your device are deleted.

EEA and UK. Where the law requires consent (we use Google's consent service to tell), stats are held on your device and not sent until you say yes. If you say no, the held stats are deleted. You can change your answer at any time with the same switch in Settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Knot Fun is free because of a few ads between knots. Ads never interrupt a knot you are working on. There is also an optional "ad break": watch one ad and the next 3 knots have no ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are limited to content rated G.

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the app may collect your IP address (which can give a general location), device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking, plus identifiers limited to the app or developer), the ads you see and tap, and basic diagnostic and performance data. Google uses this under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you buy either purchase, ads stop. From the next launch the game no longer starts Google's ad software, and you won't see the consent or tracking questions.

Consent and tracking

The game asks nothing at launch. After you win your first knot, it asks in this order:

  • Google's consent form, only where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK). You can change your choice later in the Shop under "privacy choices". If you don't agree, Google limits the ads it shows as its rules require, and we never ask about tracking.
  • Apple's tracking question on iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency). A short note from us comes first, then Apple's own prompt. If you allow tracking, Google can use your device's advertising identifier to match ads to what you like. If you ask the app not to track, the identifier is not shared. You see the same number of ads either way. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

In the EEA and UK the same moment also asks whether to share usage stats, with the switch off until you turn it on.

Purchases

Knot Fun has two optional one-time purchases: Remove Ads, and Knot Fun Complete (no ads and every theme unlocked). Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment card or address. The game only gets a confirmation of which item you bought and saves it on your device. "restore purchases" in the Shop asks Apple for that confirmation again. The game talks to Apple through Unity's in-app purchasing library, which does not collect personal data. If usage stats are on, they include purchase steps (for example opened, completed or cancelled) with the price and currency. See Apple's privacy policy.

The Unity engine

Knot Fun is built with the Unity game engine. Unity Technologies may receive basic technical information about your device, such as its model, operating system, language, graphics hardware and an app-specific device identifier, to support the engine. See Unity's player privacy policy.

Sharing knots

Share pictures and videos are made on your device and go only where you send them: saved to your photos (if you allow it) or sent through your device's share sheet. We don't upload them.

A shared knot link contains only a knot code: the difficulty, the theme and a number that rebuilds the same knot. It has nothing about you in it. If the person opening the link doesn't have the game, a small web page hosted on GitHub Pages opens. That page has no tracking of its own, but like any website host, GitHub may log the visit, including the IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Why we use your data (EEA and UK)

  • Usage stats: your consent in the EEA and UK. Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the game, with a switch to turn them off.
  • Ads: your consent through Google's form where the law requires it. Google is responsible for its own use of the data.
  • Purchases: to give you what you paid for.
  • Emails you send us: to answer you.

How long we keep it

  • Data on your device: until you delete the app. Turning stats off deletes stats waiting to be sent.
  • Usage stats: PostHog keeps them for one year on our current plan. If that changes, we will update this policy.
  • Ad data: Google keeps it under its own policies.
  • Emails: only as long as we need to help you.

Where your data is processed

Usage stats are stored by PostHog on servers in the European Union. Google and Apple process data in many countries, including the United States, under their own policies and safeguards.

Children

Knot Fun is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children and is not in the App Store's Kids category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). Ads are limited to content rated G, and usage stats are anonymous. If you think a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

  • Turn usage stats off in Settings, "share anonymous usage stats".
  • Change ad consent in the Shop, "privacy choices" (shown where the law requires it), and tracking in your device's Settings.
  • Remove ads by buying either purchase.
  • Delete everything on your device by deleting the app. The random install number lives in the app's storage, so deleting the app also cuts the link to any past stats. A new install gets a new number.
  • Ask us to access or delete analytics data by emailing adamstox@gmail.com. Because the stats are anonymous and the install number is never shown to us or to you, we usually can't tell which stats are yours. If you give us details that let us find them (such as your device model and when you played), we will delete what we can match.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Canada or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell your personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change is important, we will also mention it in the app's update notes.
